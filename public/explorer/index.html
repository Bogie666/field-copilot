How to use the trainer

  • Rotate drag · Pan right-drag / two-finger · Zoom scroll / pinch
  • Click any part (or pick from the list) to see its function, failure modes and tests. Double-click to fly to it.
  • Exploded pulls the unit apart in service order; Parts table lays every part out on the floor.
  • Guided Teardown walks through a safe disassembly. Refrigerant Flow animates the cooling cycle.
  • Keys: E explode · L callouts · X x-ray · C section · B blueprint · T turntable · R reset · Esc deselect
